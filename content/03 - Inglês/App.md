
## The Concept

A side panel / overlay that you open **while** you're in a Meet session. It guides you, gives you prompts, and tracks your practice — Meet is just the camera tool.

---

## How It Could Work

### Before the call:

- Pick topic + difficulty
- App generates speaking prompts for that session

### During the call _(companion running on the side)_:

- Shows prompt cards one by one
- Timer counting your speaking time
- Quick notes you can jot mid-session
- "Hide self-view" reminder 😄

### After the call:

- Mark what went well / what was hard
- App saves the session log
- Suggests vocabulary or topics for next time

---

## Why This Is Better

- Way simpler to build — no API headaches with Google
- Works with any video call (Meet, Zoom, Teams...)
- Feels like a real tool, not a gimmick
- You own the full experience

---

## Format Options

|Format|Pro|Con|
|---|---|---|
|Web app|Easy to build, works anywhere|Need to alt-tab|
|Chrome extension|Sits on top of Meet natively|More complex to build|
|Mobile app|Always visible on second device|Separate device needed|

> Given your stack, a **web app** is the natural starting point — and honestly, opening it on your phone while Meet runs on your laptop is a totally valid UX.
