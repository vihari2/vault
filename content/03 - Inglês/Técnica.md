
# Practicing English in Google Meet (Alone)

## Why It Works

Speaking out loud is fundamentally different from thinking in English — and being "on camera" adds just enough pressure to make it feel real.

### It helps with:

- Getting comfortable hearing your own voice in English
- Improving pronunciation through active speaking
- Simulating real conversation pressure
- Building the habit of responding without mentally translating first

---

## Tips to Make It More Effective

- **Record yourself** and watch it back — painful, but very effective
- **Pick a topic** and talk for 2–3 minutes without stopping
- **React out loud** to a video or article in English

---

## Bonus Tip

Hiding your self-view (as you asked earlier) can actually help — seeing your own face is distracting and makes you more self-conscious. Without it, you focus on just **speaking**.
