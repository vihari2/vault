
# The Social Filter

In English, we use a **social filter** to choose the best way to speak based on our relationship with the person. It is not just about being **formal** or **informal**, but about showing respect or building a connection.

---

## 1. The Polite Toolkit (Could & Would)

- Use these when you want to be extra respectful, professional, or when you are talking to someone for the first time.

| Structure | Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Would you like to...?** | Inviting someone politely | Would you like to join us for a coffee later? |
| **Could we...?** | Suggesting a time or place | Could we meet at the office on Friday? |
| **Could you please...?** | Asking for a favor or information | Could you please send me the location? |
| **Would... work?** | Checking someone's availability | Would Wednesday work for you? |

---

## 2. The Casual Toolkit (Can & How about)

- Use these for friends, family, or colleagues you talk to on a daily basis.

| Structure | Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Do you want to...?** | Inviting a friend | Do you want to grab a beer? |
| **How about...?** | Making a fast suggestion | How about Saturday at 10 am? |
| **Can you...?** | Asking for something directly | Can you meet me at the mall? |
| **Does... work?** | Checking a date quickly | Does Monday work? |
